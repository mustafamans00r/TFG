Common
questions.

How long does a typical engagement take?
Sprints run two weeks. Full projects are usually 8-16 weeks depending on scope. We can move faster when needed — ask us about our 4-week MVP track.
Do you work with companies outside Pakistan?
Yes, a significant portion of our clients are based in the US, UK, and UAE. We are used to working across different time zones asynchronously.
Who owns the work after launch?
You do. 100%. Once the final payment is cleared, all IP, source code, and design assets are transferred to your ownership.
Can you take over an existing codebase?
We usually prefer starting fresh to ensure our quality standards, but we do audit and take over existing codebases if they are built with modern, maintainable tech stacks.
